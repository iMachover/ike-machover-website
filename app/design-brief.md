Design read: A personal creative-technology homepage for visitors who should feel playfulness, curiosity, and range before reading a resume.
Concept spine: Journey/waypoints. A reflective ring travels through eleven worlds as fragments of Ike's interests.
Delivery tier: Cinema. The visitor controls a film by scrolling; expressive HTML typography responds to each location.

Journey shape: multi-leg. The original nine places expand to eleven with a hands-on computer-building chapter and an AI-assisted programming chapter before the cosmic finale. Short seam-locked legs preserve each beat instead of compressing it into one hurried film.
